STEPS TO GROW CAULIFLOWER

Step 1: Cauliflower seeds are dark brown in color and round in shape.

Step 2: Spring and summer are the best seasons to grow them.

Step 3: Choose a pot with at least 12 inches depth and 16–18 inches across.

Step 4: Fill the pot with potting soil, leaving 1/2 inch space at the top. Place four seeds in the center and cover lightly with soil.

Step 5: Keep the soil moist but not wet. Once seeds sprout, place pots near a sunny window or under a grow lamp (50–80°F).

Step 6: After seedlings reach 2 inches, thin them to one plant per pot. Give 6–8 hours of sun daily. Blanch the plant when the head is 2–3 inches across by tying leaves together over the head.

Step 7: Cauliflower takes 50–120 days to mature. Harvest when the head size is around 6–8 inches. Do not reuse the same soil as it may contain harmful organisms.

HEALTH BENEFITS OF CAULIFLOWER

1. Contains many nutrients

2. High in fiber

3. Good source of antioxidants

4. May aid weight loss

5. High in choline

6. Rich in sulforaphane

7. Low-carb alternative to grains and legumes

8. Easy to add to your diet
