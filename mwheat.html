Avoidance of Pests (Wheat)

1. Adapt the sowing time.
2. Implement crop rotation with species resistant to major wheat pests and diseases.
3. This reduces pest population and starting pathogen load (inoculum).
4. Keep the field weed-free and handle crop residues properly.
5. Thoroughly clean grain storage areas.
6. Corners, joints, and crevices harbor pests; clean these areas.
7. Pests can survive under old grain, dust, and cracks—eliminate these refuges.
