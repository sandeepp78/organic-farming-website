STEPS TO GROW CARROT

Step 1 : Plant your carrots in Spring (February - March) or in Fall (October - November).

Step 2 : Carrot plants need direct sunlight.

Step 3 : Plant carrots in soft, permeable loose soil.

Step 4 : Plant seeds about 2 inches away from each other.

Step 5 : Use a container that is at least 18 inches deep if container growing.

Step 6 : Water carrots regularly; keep soil evenly moist.

Step 7 : Protect carrots from pests and diseases.

Step 8 : Harvest carrots when mature and enjoy fresh carrots.

HEALTH BENEFITS OF CARROTS

1. Reduced risk of some cancers.

2. Lower blood cholesterol.

3. Support for weight loss and management.

4. Good for eye health (beta-carotene).

5. Helps balance blood sugar.

6. Supports immune function.

7. May boost brain health.

8. Provides vitamins and minerals for overall health.
