Avoidance of Pests (Corn)

1. Common vegetable garden contact insecticides, such as carbaryl, cyhalothrin, cypermethrin, permethrin, or bifenthrin, will adequately control caterpillars (corn earworms, fall armyworms, and European corn borers) as well as beetles (flea beetles, Japanese beetles, and sap beetles).
2. For a few corn plants, try using vegetable or corn oil mixed with Bacillus thuringiensis var. kurstaki (Btk, sold as Dipel or Thuricide) or Neem. Mix 1 part Btk or Neem with 20 parts oil for effective control.
