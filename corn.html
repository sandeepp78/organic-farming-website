STEPS TO GROW CORN

Step 1: Sow Corn seeds in well-drained soil rich in added organic manure like bio-compost or vermicompost.

Step 2: Water your potted corn plant every day in summer & every other day in winter.

Step 3: Water at the soil near the plant. Use a watering can to make sure plants get water in the form of a shower & not by a single flow. You can also make provision for watering by drip irrigation system on your raised beds.

Step 4: Add good quality organic manure in the soil with 2:1 ratio before sowing seeds.

Step 5: Organic manure can be well rotten cow dung manure, farmyard manure, Compost or Vermicompost.

Step 6: Corn plant will start flowering in the 3rd month; corn cobs can be harvested after 80–90 days of seed sowing.

Step 7: Corn cob should be harvested when seeds are well formed, but its skin can be punctured with a fingernail.

Step 8: A downward twisting jerk will usually snap the ear from the stalk.
